A DREAM Robotics Agent · Hexapod

WHIP

Walking Hexapod Intelligence Platform

Eighteen servos on an RTrobot controller, driven by an ESP32 that runs her gaits, WiFi and a browser control page. An MPU6050 stops her before she tips, and an ultrasonic sensor watches the way ahead.

Overview

Six legs, eighteen joints

Walk

Gaits

Forward, backward, turn left and turn right, plus stand, rest and shut-down poses, all tuned in the RTrobot editor.

Avoid

Obstacle mode

Walks on her own until something is within 20 cm, then stands, turns and carries on once it's clear past 35 cm.

Balance

Tilt safety

Past 10° of pitch or roll she stops and stands, and resumes once she's back under 7°.

Remote

IR remote

Arrows walk and turn while held. She stands when you let go.

Web

Browser & gamepad

A control page on port 5005, and a Python controller that drives her from a PS2-style gamepad.

Fleet

RIFT integration

She joins the NORA network and registers with the fleet, or hosts her own access point.

Hardware

What she's built from

  1. 01 ESP32 (the same module as NORA)
  2. 02 RTrobot 32-channel servo controller
  3. 03 18-DOF hexapod chassis
  4. 04 18× MG995 180° servos
  5. 05 3S LiPo battery
  6. 06 UBEC set to 6 V
  7. 07 HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor
  8. 08 MPU6050 gyro + accelerometer
  9. 09 NEC IR receiver + remote

Power

3S LiPo ──► UBEC (6 V) ──► RTrobot servo controller V+ / GND
All grounds tied together

Set the UBEC to 6 V before connecting the servos, and make sure every module shares a common ground.

The servo controller shares UART0 with the USB bridge, so disconnect it before uploading. scripts/servo_setup centres every channel at 1500 µs for fitting servo horns.

Quick start

Get her walking

  1. Flash scripts/esp32/esp32.ino with the servo controller's wires disconnected, then reconnect them.
  2. Power WHIP. She stands, joins NORA's network or starts her own WHIP AP, and starts in Obstacle Avoidance mode.
  3. Open http://<WHIP's IP>:5005/, or run the Python controller.
$ python -m venv venv
$ source venv/bin/activate
$ pip install -r requirements.txt
$ python scripts/controller.py
